Weight Loss Simulations

Two interactive demos on body composition, BMI, and GLP-1 weight loss in midlife women. Open either one, or run them in order.

Simulation 1
Same Scale, Different Body
Three women, three BMIs. Reveal what the scale can't show — muscle vs. visceral fat.
Simulation 2
Ozempic Reality Check
Toggle protein + resistance training and see what the injection actually takes off — fat or muscle.

Illustrative simulations for education only — not clinical diagnostic tools.
